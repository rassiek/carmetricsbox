Firmware & web app update

Upload CarMetrics.ino.bin (firmware) or littlefs.bin (web app) from your phone or laptop. The device reboots afterwards. Settings are kept – they live in NVS, not the filesystem.

An extra board talks to the main board over ESP-NOW and stops joining Wi-Fi networks (its own access point stays on). Pair it on the main board's Boards page.

Alerts & engine detection
Ignition sense & sleep (optional)

Feed the 12 V ignition through a divider or opto-coupler, never directly. With sleep on, the board draws well under 1 mA and wakes when the key turns on (needs an RTC-capable GPIO).

GPS module (optional)

Add GPS sensors (speed, altitude…) on the Sensors page. Without a module nothing else is affected.

Buses & misc

Turbo cool-down timer

After a run, the dashboard and OLED show the EGT and a countdown until it's safe to switch off, with a short beep. Indicator only: it never keeps the engine running by itself.

Without RPM or speed it can't tell idling from driving, so it only shows while the EGT is above the safe level.

Use a known elevation (home, a signposted pass). This sets the sea-level reference from your BMP sensor and turns off the automatic weather calibration (turn it back on in the Weather tab).

Sends downsampled metrics (average, min, max per interval) when the ESP32 has internet via Also join network. While offline they are kept in a ring buffer (RAM first, then flash) and uploaded later, oldest first.

Zabbix

InfluxDB line protocol

Fields are the sensor IDs (plus _min/_max), tagged device=<hostname>, millisecond timestamps. HTTPS is encrypted but the certificate is not verified.
